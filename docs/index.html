How it works

Three public datasets, one map.

Orbits

Positions, live.

Orbit data from CelesTrak, refreshed every three hours. Your browser computes where each satellite is, every second.

Windows

An honest path.

Re-entry times come from The Aerospace Corporation where they publish one. A prediction is hours wide, so we draw every stretch of ground it could cross, never a single pin.

People

Who lives under it.

JRC GHS-POP 2020 population, counted within 50 km of the path. Finds on the ground from ESA's recovered-debris list and a 16-language news search.
